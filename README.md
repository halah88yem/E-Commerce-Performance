 📊 E-Commerce Performance Financial Analysis

## Overview

This project presents an in-depth analysis of **e-commerce performance metrics**, focusing on sales trends, profitability, customer demographics, and regional insights. The dashboards leverage **data analytics** and **visualization tools** to uncover valuable insights that drive business decisions.

---

## Key Objectives:

- ✔ Analyze revenue, profit, and order trends over multiple years.
- ✔ Identify high-performing product categories and customer preferences.
- ✔ Assess sales distribution across regions and customer demographics.
- ✔ Provide actionable insights for business growth and optimization.

---

## DAX Measures & Calculations:

- **DAX** was used to find the maximum values, such as the most sold product.
- **DAX** was also used to calculate the changes from 2011 to 2016.
- **Functions** were used to create a new column to calculate the **Profit Margins**.

---

## Observations:

1. **Product Mix**: 
   - **Bikes** are the superstar, contributing to **78.36%** of total sales. Accessories and Clothing play smaller roles.

2. **Geographic Distribution**: 
   - The **United States** and **Australia** are your top performers in both revenue and profit, indicating a strong market presence in these countries.

3. **Revenue and Profit Trends**: 
   - Both metrics show a general **upward trend**, indicating growth. However, there was a slight decline in **2016** that may need further investigation.

4. **Order Volatility**: 
   - Significant changes in the number of orders, especially in **2013**, with a massive increase. Understanding the factors behind this surge could be crucial.

---

## Business Recommendations:

- 🔧 **Lower bike production costs** while maintaining quality.
- 🧢 **Prioritize marketing** for the **AWC Logo Cap** and explore similar product opportunities.
- 📦 **Expand** the **Accessories & Clothing** categories to diversify profits.
- 📊 **Analyze the post-2015 sales decline** and adjust strategies accordingly.
- 🎯 **Promote high-demand, lower-cost Accessories** to boost profit margins.
- 🏷️ **Implement targeted promotions** for Clothing & Accessories to balance sales distribution.
- 💻 **Strengthen online marketing efforts** for **Young Adults (25-34)** and **Adults (35-64)**.
- 🌍 **Enhance international presence** in **Canada & the U.K.**.
- 👥 **Utilize the gender-balanced audience** for customized marketing campaigns.
